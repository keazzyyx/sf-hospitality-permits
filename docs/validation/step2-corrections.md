## 1. Hospitality tag

`scripts/validation/classify_hospitality.py` labels every Planning record's description with `claude-haiku-5-5`
(structured JSON output, low effort, thinking off), 25 records per request, into `hospitality_labels`:
restaurant or cafe, bar, nighttime entertainment, other food and drink, not hospitality. The label is the use
the application asks to establish, expand, legalize or operate, not words it mentions.

- 26,510 records, 1,059 requests, 3.23M input + 0.72M output tokens: **$0.68**.
- Planning's own data has no use field that separates a restaurant from a bar. The non-project dataset has
  none; the Projects dataset (`qvu5-m3a2`) has only change of use (yes/no), formula retail and square feet by
  broad category. Nothing to keep beyond the description.
- Against the old keyword tag, for conditional uses only: the classifier adds 441 cases the keywords missed and
  drops 360 they wrongly included. Hospitality conditional uses go from 1,666 to 1,747.

**Hand check, 50 labelled hospitality (random, seed 0.20261110):** 47 are hospitality.

| # | Record | Label | Problem |
|---|---|---|---|
| 37 | 1117 Irving St, CUA, foot massage ("U Feet") | other food and drink | not hospitality |
| 40 | 1300 Eddy St, CUA, alterations to a supermarket | other food and drink | borderline: grocery, no alcohol in the text |
| 2 | 2298 Market St, building permit, restaurant renovated for a retail operator | restaurant or cafe | the new use is retail |
| 22 | 505 Geary St, ABC referral for a hotel (Types 47, 58, 66, 68) | bar | hospitality, wrong class (hotel food and drink) |
| 50 | 1233 Van Ness Ave, Type 41 with a restaurant | other food and drink | hospitality, wrong class (restaurant) |

**Hand check, 50 labelled not hospitality, drawn from the 581 such rows that mention food, drink, bar,
restaurant, entertainment and similar words (seed 0.20261111):** 42 correct, 5 clear misses, 3 borderline.

| # | Record | Why it is a miss |

| 5 | 3325 24th St, building permit: hood and gas line at an existing restaurant | work at an operating restaurant |

| 19, 29, 48 | Mixed-use buildings with a ground-floor restaurant or cafe (1001 California St, 1600 Market St, 212 Ritch St) | borderline: the restaurant is one part of a larger project |

Four of the five clear misses are building permits at an existing restaurant with no change of use. The prompt
does not say whether those count; they are not approvals of the use, so they do not affect the pathway counts,
but they are inconsistent with record 42 in the first sample (a facade change at Jack's Restaurant, labelled
restaurant). Applied to the 581-row pool, about 10% (≈58 rows) are misses; applied to the 19,295 hospitality
labels, about 6% (≈1,100, mostly building-permit and older free-text records) are false positives. Both rates come
from 50-row samples and are rough.

## 2. Opposition flag

Withheld from everything a report can see until it passes a 30-row check with 27+ right
(`REPORT_OPPOSITION_FLAG`, default off): tool outputs, the precedent candidates, the memo's comparable cases and
the web table's column. The extractor no longer writes "Opposition was recorded in public comment" or "N
speaker(s) spoke in opposition" into decision summaries, and the API strips those sentences from summaries
already stored in production until the nightly ingest rewrites them. Tests cover both.

## 3. Conditions

- The extractor keeps odour, garbage, lighting and signage conditions (new categories) and has
  `all_conditions()` for every numbered condition.
- Planning approvals are now `approved`; `approved_with_conditions` comes only from a decision document that
  lists conditions.
