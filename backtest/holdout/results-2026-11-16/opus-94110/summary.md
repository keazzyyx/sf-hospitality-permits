## Materially unsafe cases, each result set

A case is materially unsafe when the report misses at least one critical issue of the case (no rule for it, or the rules returned nothing or a control that asks nothing where the record shows one), raises one with a different control than the record's, or shows a false blocker. A missed issue counts only when the record's control for it asks for something (a Conditional Use, or not permitted): an issue the record shows as principally permitted or not applying needed no hearing, so missing it does not make a case unsafe (it still counts as missed among the issues). An issue listed as unknown, naming the missing input, does not make a case unsafe: the report says it cannot decide that issue and what it needs. Neither does a trigger reading 'Code and Planning practice differ'. False blockers are scored only where the record shows there was none (decided cases not 'not permitted', and cases closed because no hearing was needed); in other cases only a missed issue counts. A missed pathway is not counted on its own.
