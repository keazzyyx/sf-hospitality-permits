Search tips

Every word must appear, in any order, in the condition, its motion's case number or its address: a case number (2023-005418CUA) or an address (2351 Mission) finds that motion's conditions, and adding words narrows them (2351 Mission noise). A street (Valencia St, 24th St) finds every motion on it, and the conditions that name it; with its type (St, Ave) it is that street only, so 19th St is not 19th Ave. Word endings do not matter (guard, guards, guarding).
